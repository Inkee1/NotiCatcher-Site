Free access

After you choose to start free and complete server registration, all keyword alarm features are available for 168 hours. After the trial ends, a total of 2 additional alarms across all apps is provided in each fixed 48-hour window, anchored to the trial's end. Unused allowances do not roll over. Free alarms require online authorization. If the allowance is exhausted or authorization fails, no additional sound/vibration alarm is provided; the source app's notification remains. Check usage status and detection history for the result.
